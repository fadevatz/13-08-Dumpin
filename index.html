FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOT QUESTIONS? WE HAVE ANSWERS

How does dumpster delivery and pickup work?

We schedule the delivery date and time that fits your project. We drop off the dumpster directly at your job site, and as soon as it's loaded, just call or text us for fast pickup.

What types of demolition does Dumpin perform?

We demolish residential houses, concrete/wood garages, sheds, decks, porches, and small commercial structures using heavy equipment and safety-certified crews.

What dumpster size do I need for my project?

10–15 Yard dumpsters are ideal for bathroom remodels, small roof tear-offs, or light cleanouts. 20–30 Yard dumpsters are perfect for full house remodels, major cleanouts, and demolition debris.

How fast can you deliver a dumpster?

We offer fast turnaround times with Same-Day and Next-Day delivery options available throughout our service area in Greater Boston and nearby regions.
